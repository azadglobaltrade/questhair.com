A little guidance

Let's talk hair.

Everything you need to feel confident in your next great hair day.

How do I choose the right texture?

Start with the look you love and the natural texture you want to blend with. Straight textures offer a sleek finish, wavy hair adds movement, and curly or textured styles give beautiful volume. Our Find My Match guide can help you narrow it down.

What makes Quest Hair different?

Quest Hair offers distinct collections with carefully selected origins and textures. Pure is natural virgin remy hair from India; Bounce offers steam-textured Indian hair; SEA features Southeast Asian textures; and Lotus features luxurious Cambodian hair.

How do I find my perfect length?

Hair is measured when stretched straight, so curls appear shorter at the same listed length. Choose 10–14 inches for a shorter look, 16–20 inches for everyday length, or 22 inches and above for more drama. The final result depends on your height and installation.

How should I care for my extensions?

Detangle gently from the ends upward, cleanse and condition regularly, and allow hair to dry fully. Use a heat protectant before styling and protect your hair with a satin or silk wrap at night. Follow the care instructions for your specific collection.
